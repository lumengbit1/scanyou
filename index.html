Frequently asked questions

How do I scan a document?

Tap Scan on the home screen and photograph each page. You can retake or delete pages before tapping save. To scan more pages into an existing document, open it and tap Add Page.

Can I import photos or PDFs I already have?

Yes. Tap Import, then choose Photos or Files (PDF or image). Photos of documents are cropped automatically, and each PDF page becomes a page in ScanYou.

The crop isn't quite right. How do I fix it?

Open the page, tap Crop and drag the four corners. The original photo is always kept, so you can re-crop at any time or choose Full image.

How do I remove shadows or make text clearer?

Open a page and tap Filters. Auto works well for most documents; Unshade removes shadows and Contrast sharpens text. Use Erase to remove marks or smudges. Any edit can be undone.

Why can't search find a word I can see?

Search uses the text ScanYou recognises on each page. If you've just changed a filter or crop, the page needs to be recognised again: open the document and tap Recognize again. Very small, blurry or decorative text may not be recognised.

How do I export a PDF?

Open a document and tap Export. Choose PDF, JPG, PNG or TXT. Searchable PDFs include the recognised text. Under More settings you can pick the paper size, orientation, margins and quality.

I deleted a document by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, if it's still in the Recycle bin (Settings > Recycle bin). You can choose to keep deleted documents for 30 days, 90 days or until you remove them yourself.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Your documents, images and their text are stored on your iPhone, and text recognition happens on the device. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I turn usage statistics on or off?

Go to Settings > Usage statistics. Statistics are optional and never include document contents, text or file names.
