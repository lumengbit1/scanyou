Frequently asked questions

What does Intelligent analysis do?

It reads a document for you. Open a document and tap Intelligent analysis, then Analyze document. ScanYou suggests a name, a category and a matching folder, writes a short summary and key points, and pulls out details such as dates, amounts and names, each with the page it came from. You can also tap Describe a page image to get a description of a photo or diagram on a page.

Everything runs on your device with Apple's on-device model. Your documents are not uploaded. Results can be wrong, so check them before you apply a name or folder.

Why does Intelligent analysis say it's not available on my device?

Intelligent analysis needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence, for example iPhone 15 Pro or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri. After you turn it on, the model may take a while to download. On other devices the rest of ScanYou works as usual.

How do I erase handwriting, stamps or marks?

Open a page and tap Erase. Paint over what you want to remove with Fine, Medium or Broad, then tap Preview repair. ScanYou fills the area from the surrounding paper or texture. Choose Paper for text and marks, Texture repair for small objects, or leave it on Auto. If you change your mind later, use Restore before erase.

How do I merge documents or split out pages?

To merge, tap Select on the home screen, choose two or more documents, then tap Merge documents and Create new document. To split, open a document, tap Select above the pages, choose the pages you want, then use More actions to save them as a new document. Crops, filters, annotations and recognised text come with the pages, and the original documents stay unchanged.

How do I scan a document?

Tap Scan on the home screen and photograph each page. You can retake or delete pages before tapping save. To scan more pages into an existing document, open it and tap Add Page.

Can I import photos or PDFs I already have?

Yes. Tap Import, then choose Photos or Files (PDF or image). Photos of documents are cropped automatically, and each PDF page becomes a page in ScanYou.

The crop isn't quite right. How do I fix it?

Open the page, tap Crop and drag the four corners. The original photo is always kept, so you can re-crop at any time or choose Full image.

How do I remove shadows or make text clearer?

Open a page and tap Filters. Auto works well for most documents; Unshade removes shadows and Contrast sharpens text. Use Erase to remove marks or smudges. Any edit can be undone.

Why can't search find a word I can see?

Search uses the text ScanYou recognises on each page. If you've just changed a filter or crop, the page needs to be recognised again: open the document and tap Recognize again. Very small, blurry or decorative text may not be recognised.

How do I export a PDF?

Open a document and tap Export. Choose PDF, JPG, PNG or TXT. Searchable PDFs include the recognised text. Under More settings you can pick the paper size, orientation, margins and quality.

I deleted a document by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, if it's still in the Recycle bin (Settings > Recycle bin). You can choose to keep deleted documents for 30 days, 90 days or until you remove them yourself.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Your documents, images and their text are stored on your iPhone or iPad, and text recognition and Intelligent analysis happen on the device. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I turn usage statistics and crash reports on or off?

Go to Settings > Usage statistics. Statistics and crash reports share one setting, are off until you allow them, and never include document contents, text or file names.
