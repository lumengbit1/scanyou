Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

ScanYou ("the app") is an on-device document assistant and scanner for iPhone and iPad, developed by Meng Lu ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored and the choices you have. In short: your documents stay on your device, there is no account, and usage statistics are optional.

Your documents

Scans, imported images and PDFs, the text recognised from them, document names, folders and edit history are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We do not upload them to our servers, and we do not have servers that receive them. Your device's backups (such as iCloud Backup) may include this data, depending on your device settings.

Text recognition and the optional Intelligent analysis run entirely on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks. Intelligent analysis is available on devices that support Apple Intelligence; it suggests a name, category and folder, writes a summary and key points, extracts details such as dates and amounts with their page numbers, and can describe page images. Its results are saved with the document on your device and are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Documents leave your device only when you choose to share or export them, for example to Files, Mail or another app. What happens next is governed by that destination.

Permissions

Camera

Used to photograph documents. Photos are kept in the app; they are not added to your photo library.

Photos and Files

When you import, the app reads only the photos or files you pick.

Location (optional)

Off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, the app uses a photo's location, or your current location while you use the app, to add the suburb and city to the names of new scans. To turn coordinates into a place name, the app uses Apple's geocoding service, which receives those coordinates under Apple's Privacy Policy. The place name and coordinates are saved with the document on your device.

Usage statistics and crash reports (optional)

With your permission, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how features are used, and Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes. Crash reports follow the same choice as statistics. Statistics are off until you allow them, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Usage statistics. If you turn them off, collection stops, the analytics data stored on your device is reset and crash reports not yet sent are deleted. A change to crash reporting applies from the next time the app starts.

When allowed, the app sends:

  • screen names and actions, such as "scan completed" or "PDF exported";
  • counts and durations, such as the number of pages and how long an export took;
  • outcomes and general error types;
  • app and device information, such as app version, iOS version and device model;
  • an app-instance identifier created by Firebase;
  • if the app crashes, a crash report: where in the app's code the crash happened, and the device's state at that moment, such as free memory and disk space and whether the app was in the foreground.

Statistics and crash reports never include document contents, images, recognised text, document or file names, document identifiers or your location. Advertising features are disabled: the app does not collect the advertising identifier, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not share data with data brokers. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy; Google may derive an approximate region from your IP address.

Ratings

The app may occasionally ask the system to show Apple's rating prompt. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple.

Data retention and deletion

Your documents remain on your device until you delete them. Deleted documents go to the Recycle bin and are removed after the period you choose (30 or 90 days), or when you remove them yourself. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Analytics data held by Google is kept according to Google Analytics retention settings, and crash reports are kept for 90 days; to ask us to request deletion of analytics data or crash reports, contact us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date above.
